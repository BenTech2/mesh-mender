      Repair

      Welds vertices, removes junk triangles, separates pinched geometry, fixes winding and fills every hole.

      Choose individual fixes

      Cuts out the triangles that pass through each other and re-closes the gap. Surgical: it only touches a body crossing itself, and leaves the rest of the model exactly as it is.

      Experimental

      Re-derives the surface from scratch instead of editing it. Fixes intersections that cannot be edited away, and merges overlapping bodies into one solid. Every detail is resampled, so sharp edges soften and the triangle count goes up a lot.
